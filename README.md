# Physics + ANN for Data Center Cooling Energy — Article Review

A short HTML slideshow reviewing:

> Li, X., Wang, X., He, Z., Chen, X., & Li, Z. (2024). Combining physical laws and ANN for predicting energy consumption of data center cooling systems. *Energy and Buildings*, 311, Article 114170. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2024.114170

Prepared as a University of Ottawa student presentation.

## View it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

**Controls:** ← / → (or click / swipe) to navigate · **N** speaker notes · **F** fullscreen · add `#5` to the URL to jump to slide 5.
